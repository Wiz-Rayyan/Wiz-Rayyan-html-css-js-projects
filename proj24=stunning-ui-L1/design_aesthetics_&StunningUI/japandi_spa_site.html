The practice

A day spent in
intentional quiet

Maki follows the natural rhythm of light and water. No clocks, no schedules — only a gentle progression through states of warmth, stillness, and renewal.

Morning arrival & tea

Welcomed with hojicha and a small bowl of seasonal fruit. A moment to release the outside world.

Thermal bathing sequence

Move through heat, steam, and cold at your own pace. No instruction, no interruption.

Bodywork or stillness

Choose a treatment or simply rest in the garden room with a book and filtered light.

Evening closing

A light meal of pickled vegetables, dashi broth, and brown rice before departing into dusk.
