What guides the work

Light

Every piece is designed around how northern light moves through a room across a day — soft in the morning, low and warm by evening.

Material

We work only with alder, ash, and oak sourced within 200km, finished in oil rather than lacquer so the wood can keep aging honestly.

Quiet

No piece should compete for attention. If a chair is the most interesting thing in the room, we haven't finished designing it yet.

The signature piece

One chair, four finishes — hover to re-finish it by eye.

The Alder Chair is steam-bent from a single piece of timber. Its finish is the only thing that changes from home to home; the shape stays the same.
