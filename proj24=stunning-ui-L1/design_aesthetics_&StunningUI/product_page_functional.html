Can I use these while charging?
Yes — plug in USB-C and listen via 3.5mm simultaneously. Fast-charge gives 5 hours of playback in 15 minutes.
Does LDAC work on iPhone?
LDAC is Android-only. iPhone users get AAC, which still delivers excellent quality — you'll only notice the gap in careful A/B testing on hi-res source files.
What's covered under warranty?
Two years against manufacturing defects. Battery degradation beyond 20% in year one is also covered. Plus a 30-day no-questions return window from date of purchase.
Can I replace the ear cushions?
Yes, they snap off with a quarter-turn. Replacement cushions in memory foam or vegan leather are available separately.
Can I connect to two devices at once?
Yes, multipoint Bluetooth lets you stay connected to a laptop and phone simultaneously. Audio pauses on one device when the other starts playing.
