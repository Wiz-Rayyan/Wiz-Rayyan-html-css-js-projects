Based between Rome and the Umbrian hills, I work primarily in watercolor and ink on cold-press cotton paper. My practice is rooted in observational drawing — the garden, the figure, the worn surface of old things.


Original works and limited archival prints are available for purchase. Commissions open seasonally.
