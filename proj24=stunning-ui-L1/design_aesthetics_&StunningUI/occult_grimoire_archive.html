Codex Arcanum · The Grand Cipher

The Cosmological Circle of Working

The master diagram of the hidden art: concentric rings mark the seven spheres of influence, from the sublunary to the empyrean. The inner seal contains that which is summoned; the outer ring seals against intrusion from without.

Each nodal point corresponds to one of the seven planetary hours. Ritual must commence when the governing sphere is in ascendancy, lest the working be scattered to the winds and the practitioner left exposed.
