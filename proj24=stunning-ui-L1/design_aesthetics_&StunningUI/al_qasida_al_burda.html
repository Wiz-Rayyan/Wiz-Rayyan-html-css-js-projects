Sharaf al-Din Muhammad al-Busiri · شَرَفُ الدِّين مُحَمَّد البُوصِيري Born 608 AH / 1213 CE in Dalās, Egypt. He composed the Burda during a grave illness, having seen the Prophet ﷺ in a dream who draped his mantle over him — upon waking, al-Busiri was cured. The poem was completed 7th century AH and has been illuminated, memorized, and recited continuously ever since.
Completed in Damietta · Dated 694 AH
