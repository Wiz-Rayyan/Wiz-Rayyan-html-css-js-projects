Every town has the same gas station. I keep stopping anyway.

Flagstaff. Barstow. Needles. Gallup. The logos change but the layout never does: coolers along the back wall, candy by the register, the smell of old coffee and synthetic cherry. I have photographs from a dozen of them. They could all be the same place.

slurpee theory

Blue raspberry is a color that doesn't exist in nature. That's the point.

There are no blue raspberries. The flavor was invented — an industrial approximation of nothing real, packaged in a cup the color of a swimming pool. At 3 AM, after six hours of highway, it tastes like exactly what you need it to taste like.
