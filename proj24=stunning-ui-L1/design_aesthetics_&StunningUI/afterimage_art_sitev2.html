Negative persistence / phosphor burn / optical residue

01 / MECHANISM
Photoreceptor fatigue
Cone cells saturate under sustained stimulation. When stimulus is removed, complementary signals dominate briefly, producing the inverse color ghost.
02 / HISTORY
Op Art 1960s
Bridget Riley, Victor Vasarely, and Richard Anuszkiewicz weaponized afterimage as compositional material. The viewer's nervous system completes the work.
03 / DIGITAL
Burn-in as medium
OLED ghost retention, CRT phosphor decay, and LCD image persistence — industrial failure conditions reclaimed as aesthetic material.
