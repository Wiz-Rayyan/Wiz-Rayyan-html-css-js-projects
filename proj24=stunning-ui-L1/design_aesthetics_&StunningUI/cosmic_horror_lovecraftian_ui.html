A Cartography of Azathoth's Domain

The surveyors who returned from the nuclear chaos at the center of all things could not agree on any dimension except the sound — a piping, discordant, and utterly meaningless melody played by the mindless dancers who cavort at the throne's edge. Their maps, transcribed in the weeks before each man's faculties collapsed entirely, describe a geography of pure negation: places defined only by what they refuse to be.
