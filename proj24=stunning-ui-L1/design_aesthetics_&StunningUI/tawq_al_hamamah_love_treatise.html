Lover's Map of Cordoba — Kharitat al-Hubb
River Guadalquivir — Wadi al-Kabir
Full Moon, Rajab 402 AH · 1012 CE
Under a moon so full it cast shadows like noon, two lovers met on the eastern bank of the great river, using the reflection of a candle in the water as a signal. Ibn Hazm records that they stood at opposite banks and read each other's lips across the current — the river itself bearing witness to their constancy.
