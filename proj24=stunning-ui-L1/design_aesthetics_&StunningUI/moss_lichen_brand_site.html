Our philosophy
"What the forest keeps for years, we ask it to share only when it offers."

We do not extract. We observe, we wait, and we gather only surplus. Every batch is dated to a season and a place. Nothing here moves fast — not the growing, not the making, not the understanding of what each plant can offer.
