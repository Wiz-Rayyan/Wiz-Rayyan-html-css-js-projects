Beauty lives

We repair broken ceramics with urushi lacquer and powdered gold, then make new pieces built to break well. Every bowl leaves the kiln unfinished — the years finish it.

On Wabi-Sabi
A cup does not fail
by cracking. It only begins
its second life.

Wabi-sabi asks us to stop chasing symmetry. Our potters leave thumbprints in the clay, let glazes pool unevenly, and repair what breaks in gold rather than hiding the scar. Nothing here is finished. Everything here is honest.

How a piece is mended

Five steps, one seam

一

Break

The piece cracks — in the kiln, or in your hands.

二

Gather

Every fragment is kept, even the dust.

三

Bind

Urushi lacquer, drawn from tree sap, holds the edges.

四

Gild

Powdered gold is dusted into the wet lacquer seam.

五

Return

The piece goes back to the table, scar visible, whole again.
