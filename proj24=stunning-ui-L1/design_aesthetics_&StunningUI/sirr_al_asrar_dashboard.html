On the Virtue of Justice
Chapter III · Statecraft
Know, O Alexander, that justice is the foundation upon which kingdoms endure. As water sustains the tree, so justice sustains the throne. Let no favoritism cloud your judgment, for a ruler who departs from equity departs from the blessing of heaven. Weigh each matter as you would weigh gold: with care, with precision, and without haste.
— Aristotle to Alexander, Sirr al-Asrar
