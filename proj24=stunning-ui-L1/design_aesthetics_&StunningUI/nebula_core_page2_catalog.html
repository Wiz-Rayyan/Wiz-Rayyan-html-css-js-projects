Featured Object · NGC 7293

The Helix Nebula —
Eye of God

At only 650 light-years from Earth, the Helix is the closest large planetary nebula to our solar system. A sun-like star at its center exhaled its outer layers across 2.87 light-years of space over the last 10,600 years, ionizing the gas into the brilliant rings we observe today.
