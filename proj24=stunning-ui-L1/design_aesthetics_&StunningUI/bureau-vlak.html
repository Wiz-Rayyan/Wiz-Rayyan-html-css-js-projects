The Studio

Founded in 2018 by Merel de Boer and Jip Kramer, Bureau Vlak works out of a converted print shop in Rotterdam-West.

We don't have a signature look — every client gets a system built from their own material, not ours. What stays constant is the confidence to commit to one bold idea and cut everything else away.
