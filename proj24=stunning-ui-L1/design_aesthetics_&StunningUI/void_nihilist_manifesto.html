nothing preceded you.
nothing will follow.
the universe did not
notice your arrival
and will not mark
your absence.

meaning is a story
told by nervous systems
afraid of the dark.
the dark was always
already here.

your hopes:
electrochemical events.
your loves:
electrochemical events.
this sentence:
electrochemical events.

civilizations rose.
civilizations fell.
the void
did not care then.
it does not care now.

nothing matters.
nothing has ever
mattered.
nothing will.
