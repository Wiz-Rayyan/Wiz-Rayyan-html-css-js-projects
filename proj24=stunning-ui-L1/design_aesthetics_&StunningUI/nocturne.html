Heritage

Laid down in silence, released once

In the autumn of 2001, a single run of spirit was sealed into Spanish oak beneath our cellar's northern wall, where the temperature never wavers and the light never reaches. It has rested there since, untouched, unblended, unhurried.

Every bottle in this release comes from that one cask. When it is gone, Nocturne no longer exists.

"We did not make this spirit. We waited for it."
Tasting notes

What twenty-five years leaves behind

N

Nose

Dark plum and scorched fig, folded into cedar and a trace of church incense.

P

Palate

Rich toffee and blood orange give way to clove, leather, and warm stone.

F

Finish

Long and dry, closing on cracked walnut and a whisper of dark chocolate.

The cellar

Twenty-five years, one cask

2001

Cask laid down

Filled at first light and moved to the cellar's coldest corner, where it would not be touched again for a generation.

2010

First sampling

A single glass drawn to check its progress. The cellar master's notes read only: "not yet."

2019

The angels' share

Nearly forty percent of the original volume had been lost to evaporation, concentrating what remained.

2026

Bottled and numbered

Eight hundred bottles drawn from the cask, each hand-numbered, then the cask itself was retired.

800 bottles, worldwide

An allocation, not a purchase

Bottles are offered by request to those on our list. There is no second release, and no reprint of the label.
