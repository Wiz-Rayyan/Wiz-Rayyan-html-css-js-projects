Why KP9

Three ways to stay ahead of the storm.

Aurora chasers usually find out a substorm has already peaked. KP9 is built around the 27-minute gap between L1 satellite data and the light actually reaching your sky.

Real-time forecast

Solar wind speed, density and Bz orientation from ACE and DSCOVR, reprojected onto your latitude every 60 seconds.

Generative soundscapes

A drone that shifts key with Kp, bends pitch with Bz, and speeds up with solar wind — the storm, translated into sound.

Substorm alerts

A push the moment onset is detected in the magnetometer chain — not after the display has already faded.
