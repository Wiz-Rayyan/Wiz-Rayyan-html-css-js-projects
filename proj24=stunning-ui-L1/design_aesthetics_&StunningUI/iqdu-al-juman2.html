Verified isnād, Cairo 748–810 AH
Rajab 762 AH · Black Death
The chronicler Ibn Kathīr al-Dimashqī records the great plague. Transmission chain: al-Mizzī → al-Dhahabī → al-Birzālī → al-Ṣafadī. Of the 52 scholars in Cairo, only 19 survived to attest the events.

Muḥarram 784 AH · Accession
Sultan Barqūq al-Circassī assumes the throne in Cairo. Chancery seal № ٤٢ is issued under the hand of the head secretary. Investiture witnessed by the Qāḍī of the four rites.
Dhū al-Ḥijja 801 AH · Timur
Account of Tīmūr Lang's advance upon Syria. The chronicler al-Maqrīzī notes: three pearl-chains of transmission for this account, from eyewitnesses in Damascus and Aleppo.
✦ Biographic Pearl — Medallion ٣ ✦ أَبُو الْفِدَاء Geographer, historian, and Sultan of Ḥamāh. Died 732 AH. His isnad comprises 12 verified transmitters; his geographical compendium was read in 7 chanceries.

Dīwān al-Inshāʾ · Official Rescript
Formal correspondence opens with a pearl-dotted بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ, followed by the chain of seven secretaries. Marginal annotation: "تتمة العقد" — necklace continuation.
Tax Registry · 790 AH
Province al-Sharqiyya — Eastern Delta. Administrator: Amīr Aybak al-Bahnasāwī. Yield: 12,400 ardabb.
Villages 47 settlements recorded. Each entry bears a biographic medallion of its nāẓir (inspector).
Seal Countersigned by the kātib al-sirr, private secretary to the Sultan.
Biographical Notice · al-Qalqashandī
Author of Ṣubḥ al-Aʿshā, the great encyclopaedia of chancery arts. Describes 14 grades of siyaqat script, pearl-notation for numerals, and the hierarchy of secretarial seals.
