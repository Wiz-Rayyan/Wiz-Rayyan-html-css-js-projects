▶ PHOSPHOR DREAMS [by xX_GHOST_Xx, 1994]
The cursor blinks like a pulse
    between us—
two terminals, one handshake,
    modem sighing its long vowel
        into the dark.

You type     I read
I breathe    you wait

      C O N N E C T E D

Bytes travel the phone line
warm as breath on a nape—
28.8 kbps
        of longing.

 WHAT IS ASCII EROTICA?

 Before the internet had images, desire found form
 in characters. Artists on BBS systems worldwide
 composed suggestive, playful, and intimate images
 using nothing but the 128 characters of ASCII.

 The aesthetic is the constraint: monospace grids,
 characters repurposed as line and shadow, the
 imagination filling what pixels could not.

 This is TEXT as BODY. SYMBOL as SUGGESTION.
 The oldest digital art form.
