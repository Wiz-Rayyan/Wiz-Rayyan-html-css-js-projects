Can I use these while charging?
Yes — plug in via USB-C and listen through the 3.5 mm port simultaneously. Fast-charge gives you 5 hours of playback in just 15 minutes.
Does LDAC work on iPhone?
LDAC is Android-only. iPhone users get AAC, which still delivers excellent quality — you'll notice the difference only in A/B testing on high-resolution source files.
What's covered under warranty?
Two years against manufacturing defects, plus a 30-day no-questions return window. Battery degradation beyond 20% in year one is also covered.
Can I replace the ear cushions?
Yes, they snap off with a quarter-turn. Replacement cushions (memory foam or vegan leather) are available in the accessories section.
