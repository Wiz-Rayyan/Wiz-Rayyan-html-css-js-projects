Why illusions work

Prediction engine

The brain does not passively receive input — it predicts. Illusions exploit the gap between prediction and raw signal.

Two-stream theory

Ventral ("what") and dorsal ("where") pathways process separately. Impossible objects break their reconciliation.

Figure–ground

Gestalt law: the visual system assigns one region as object, one as background. It cannot hold both simultaneously.

Interference

Moiré arises from aliasing — a mathematical beat frequency between two periodic signals. No trickery needed.
