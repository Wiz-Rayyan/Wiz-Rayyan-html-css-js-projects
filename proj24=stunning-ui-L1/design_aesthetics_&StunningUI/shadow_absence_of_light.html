Studio · Est. mmxxiv

The shape
of what
is absent

We do not illuminate. We reveal the edge where light stops. Form is the shadow's promise of a thing that was.

I Negative space

The cave wall only shows us shadows. We have always mistaken them for the world.

II Absence as form

Cut the light away cleanly enough and what remains is a silhouette sharper than any outline.

III Penumbra

The soft edge between shadow and shadow. Where form dissolves into the field it came from.

"We do not draw the figure. We draw the darkness around it, and let the figure remain."

— On negative space, 1931
