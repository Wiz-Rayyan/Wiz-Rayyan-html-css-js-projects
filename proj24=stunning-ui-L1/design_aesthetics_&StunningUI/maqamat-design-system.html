§ II — Cards

Overlapping Manuscript Folios

§ IV — Avatars

Turban-Ranked Profiles

Avatars rendered in profile view with a single visible eye, as in al-Wasiti's figures. Turban color encodes social rank: indigo for scholar, verdigris for merchant, vermilion for poet, saffron for scribe.

§ VI — Typography

Script Hierarchy

The Assemblies of al-Hariri

In the city of Basra there was a man of eloquence and wit, named Abu Zayd of Saruj, who could move men to laughter and tears with the same breath. The narrator al-Harith ibn Hammam encountered him first in the great market, where crowds pressed against the brick arcade and oil-smoke hung in diagonals from every lamp.

Body: Amiri (Naskh style) — flowing, generous x-height, suited to long prose passages.
Headings: Amiri Bold — evokes the angular rigidity of Kufic script with diagonal baseline treatment.
