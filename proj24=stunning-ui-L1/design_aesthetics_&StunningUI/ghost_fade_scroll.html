Ghost Fade — interactive scroll demo where UI fades to nothing as you scroll down

Experimental · 2026

Everything here will disappear.

You are reading this now. The words are solid, present, legible. If you keep scrolling, they will not be. This is not a metaphor. This is the interface.

"We build things so permanent. Nothing is."

Designers obsess over visibility, clarity, contrast. But what happens when the act of looking — the act of scrolling forward — erases the thing you came for?

opacity: 1 − t   ·   blur: t × 20px   ·   translateY: t × −200px

There is something true about this. Attention is finite. Presence is not guaranteed. The further you go, the less you carry with you.

By the time you reach here, you are almost gone. The text is almost gone. What remains is a kind of negative space — the shape of something that was once readable.

Scroll slowly. Notice when it starts.

This is the last thing you will be able to read.
