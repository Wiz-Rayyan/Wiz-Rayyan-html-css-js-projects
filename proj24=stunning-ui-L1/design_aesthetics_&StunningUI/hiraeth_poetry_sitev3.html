The Longing

I am a stranger in a land I know by heart,
a pilgrim of the almost-remembered,
reaching through mist for the warmth
of fires that never quite burned.


There is a hill I have never climbed
yet stand upon in dreams —
a language in the stones
my blood already speaks.


Hiraeth — not the loss of what was,
but the ache for what could not be,
for the home the soul built
before the body arrived.

— an unnamed valley, twilight, always

Nostalgia's Ghost

It is not the past you mourn. It is the version of yourself who might have lived there, in that golden, imagined elsewhere.
