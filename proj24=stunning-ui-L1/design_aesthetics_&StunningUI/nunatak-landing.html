HOW WE VERIFY

Cold chamber first. Ice sheet second.

Every garment spends 200 hours in a calibrated cold chamber before it leaves the lab, cycled between static cold and simulated wind load.

Only after passing chamber thresholds does a prototype go to the field team — three to eight weeks logged on active expeditions before any design is finalized.
