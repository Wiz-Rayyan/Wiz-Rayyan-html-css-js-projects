Each of the 52 epistles is assigned a cosmic kingdom, a numerical value, and a marginal gloss visible on hover. The four major divisions — mathematical, natural, psychological-theological, esoteric — correspond to the four kingdoms.

On Arithmetic

Mathematical Sciences · Part I
From unity flows all multiplicity. Number is the form of forms; one is not a number but the source of number itself.
Mineral ابجد = 9
MARGINAL GLOSSUnity precedes being. The monad is the origin and end of all numerical procession.

On Music

Mathematical Sciences · Part I
The harmony of the spheres is the archetype of earthly music. Ratios of 4:3, 3:2, and 2:1 govern cosmic motion and the soul's ascent.
Vegetal موسيقى = 256
MARGINAL GLOSSMusic is philosophy made audible. Each interval corresponds to a degree of the soul's return.

On Animals

Natural Sciences · Part II
Life arises when four humours balance. The animal soul perceives, moves, and desires — distinguishing it from mineral and vegetal existence.
Animal حيوان = 75
MARGINAL GLOSSThe four humours are microcosmic mirrors of the four elements in their bodily expression.

On the Intellect

Psychological-Theological · Part III
The rational soul reflects the Universal Intellect as a mirror reflects light. Through philosophy, the seeker ascends the cosmic hierarchy toward unity.
Human عقل = 170
MARGINAL GLOSSPhilosophy and revelation are twin ladders to the same summit. The Brethren hold both in one hand.
