Veil of the Hollow Star

★ Illusion · 7th Circle ★

You weave shadows into living cloth, draping a willing creature in a shroud that confounds all mortal sight. For the duration, the subject exists between worlds — perceived as smoke, as memory, as something that was never quite there.

Casting1 action
Range60 ft
Duration8 hours
ComponentsV · S · M
