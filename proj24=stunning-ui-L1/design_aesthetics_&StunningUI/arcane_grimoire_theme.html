⁕ Spell of the Waning Moon ⁕

Umbra Vinculum

School of Shadow Binding · Rank VII

A potent enchantment drawn from the void between worlds. The caster weaves threads of shadow into unbreakable chains that bind both body and spirit, trapping the target within a shroud of eternal twilight until the caster's will is satisfied or dawn breaks thrice.

Casting Time 1 Full Moon
Components V, S, M
Duration 3 Dawns
Range 60 Feet

Elemental Rites

Master the four primal forces — flame, tide, gale, and stone — through ancient ceremonial workings passed down from the first mages.

Astral Navigation

Chart a course through the planes of existence using star-charts inked in the blood of prophets, tracing paths unseen by mortal eyes.

Sigil Crafting

Inscribe intent into reality itself. Each sigil is a focused prayer, a compressed will given form through the sacred geometry of power.

I

The First Codex

Study the foundational runes before attempting any binding. Without proper grounding in the old tongue, your words dissolve into the ether without effect.

II

The Rite of Inscription

Every spell must be written by hand, under candlelight, with ink prepared during the appropriate lunar phase. No modern transcription shall hold the necessary resonance.

III

The Covenant of Return

All power drawn from the void must be returned threefold. The mage who forgets this axiom invites ruin upon their house for seven generations hence.
