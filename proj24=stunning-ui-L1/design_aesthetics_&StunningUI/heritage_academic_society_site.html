Since its foundation in the reign of Queen Victoria, the Aldrich Society has served as a refuge for those committed to the enduring pursuit of classical scholarship. Our halls have welcomed poets, historians, and natural philosophers from every corner of the civilised world.

Membership is by election, conferring upon its recipients the privileges of our library, annual symposia, and the fellowship of five generations of dedicated scholars.

From the President's Desk
"Knowledge is not merely accumulated — it is tended, like a garden, against the encroachment of the age."
