The Evening

Three Rites of the Night

The evening unfolds in three movements, each held in a different chamber of the cathedral, each governed by its own small ceremony.

The Unmasking Hall

Guests arrive at nine, masked and unnamed, and are received by candlelight beneath the vaulted nave.

The Crimson Table

At midnight, a private supper is laid beneath the rose window — seven courses, poured by hand, spoken of by no one.

The Last Waltz

The night closes with a single dance beneath the bells, and guests depart before the first hour of morning tolls.
