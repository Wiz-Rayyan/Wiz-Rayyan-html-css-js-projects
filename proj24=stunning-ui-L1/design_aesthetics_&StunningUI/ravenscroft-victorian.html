Our Story

Founded in the winter of 1887 by Josiah Ravenscroft, a chemist turned distiller, the house began as a single cellar beneath a Bloomsbury apothecary. What started as a remedy for chill soon became a reputation for spirits so carefully aged that physicians and dukes alike sought them out in equal measure.

Three generations of the Ravenscroft and Thorne families have since kept the same oak casks, the same wax seals, and the same conviction: that a spirit worth drinking is a spirit worth waiting for. Nothing leaves the cellar before its time.

Today the house still bottles by hand, still seals each cork in burgundy wax, and still answers, when asked how long a bottle has aged, with the only true reply — long enough.
