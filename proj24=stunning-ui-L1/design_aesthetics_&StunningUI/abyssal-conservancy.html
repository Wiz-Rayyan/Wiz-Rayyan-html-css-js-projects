Ninety percent of the ocean is dark. We think that's exactly why it matters.

Most conservation funding stops at the sunlit reef. Below the twilight zone, ecosystems that took millions of years to form are being surveyed for mining rights before they're even fully named.

Abyssal Conservancy places independent observers on survey vessels, funds baseline biodiversity studies, and pushes for protected status on vent fields and seamounts still unmapped on any chart.
