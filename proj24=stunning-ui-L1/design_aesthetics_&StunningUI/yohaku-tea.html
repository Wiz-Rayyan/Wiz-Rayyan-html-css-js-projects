Tea steeped in the space between things.

Yohaku means the deliberate emptiness in a painting, a room, a breath. We grow and blend tea the same way — leaving room for the leaf, the season, and the hour to speak for themselves.

一 期 一 会 — one meeting, once in a lifetime

Three ideas guide every batch we make.

侘 · wabi

Beauty in the modest and the weathered. We don't polish away a leaf's imperfections — they're where the flavor lives.

寂 · sabi

The grace of time passing. Our teas are aged, not rushed, and taste different in April than they do in November.

間 · ma

The pause between pours. A good cup asks you to sit still for a few minutes — that's not a side effect, it's the point.
