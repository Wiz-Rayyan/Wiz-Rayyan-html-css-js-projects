This week's memory

The kitchen always smelled like cardamom and burnt toast

There are certain smells that live outside of time. You catch a trace of them on a Tuesday morning in a city far from home, and suddenly you are eight years old again, sitting at a table that no longer exists, in a house that has since been painted over, talking to people who have since moved on. Memory doesn't preserve — it transforms. Every recollection is a copy of a copy, softened at the edges, warmer in hue.
