Q3 Financial Summary
Revenue overview for the quarter ending September 30, 2024. Total revenue for Q3 reached $4.2B representing a 17% YoY increase, primarily driven by cloud segment growth and enterprise subscription renewals. Operating margin expanded 180bps to 24.3%.
Risk Factors
The following risks have been identified for Q4. Management expects supply chain headwinds to compress gross margins by 200–300bps in Q4 2024, which will be monitored closely by the finance committee.
Meeting Notes — Oct 14
Attendees: CEO, CFO, VP Sales, VP Engineering. CFO highlighted APAC region growth of 31%, significantly outpacing EMEA (12%) and Americas (14%) for the quarter. New partnerships cited as primary driver.
Action Items
Follow-ups assigned after the October meeting. Action item: Finance to model FX sensitivity scenarios for EUR/USD at 1.05 and 1.10 levels by EOQ. Owner: CFO. Due: Nov 1.
