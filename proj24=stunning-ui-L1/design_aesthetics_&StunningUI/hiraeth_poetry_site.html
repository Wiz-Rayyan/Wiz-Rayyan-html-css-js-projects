A poem of longing
The Country That Was Never There

I know the smell of its morning —
damp stone and woodsmoke,
the low call of something I once loved.

I have never stood there.
It does not exist on any map.
And yet my bones know the road.

This is hiraeth: not grief for what was lost,
but for what was never yours to lose —
the ache of an impossible home.

— Anon. · Translated from longing
