Issue XII — Silence

What remains
after the word
has left the room

A collection of poems written in the space between breath and language, between knowing and saying.

I

Still Life with Absence

the cup
still warm
from your hands

II

Negative Space

I have learned
to read
what you don't say

III

After the Door

the room holds
the shape
of your leaving
