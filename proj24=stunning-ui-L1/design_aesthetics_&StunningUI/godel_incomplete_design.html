On the incompleteness of this design
"This design is not aesthetically pleasing."
i. Assume the statement is true. Then the design is ugly. But a design of such proportions, geometry, and care cannot be ugly. Contradiction.
ii. Assume the statement is false. Then the design is beautiful. But then its central claim is false, undermining its own integrity. Contradiction.
iii. Therefore no consistent aesthetic judgment of this design is possible within the system the design itself defines. The proposition is undecidable.
∎ Q.E.D.
