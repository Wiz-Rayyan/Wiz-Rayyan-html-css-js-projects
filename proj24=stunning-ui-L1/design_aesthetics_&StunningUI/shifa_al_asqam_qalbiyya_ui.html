Prescription
✦ Remedy for Kibr & Khafaqan ✦
As prescribed by the Chief Physician Abū ʿAlī ibn Sīnā
Cause (Sabab)
Excess of yellow bile inflaming the pneuma vitalis; concurrent hardening of the spiritual heart through sustained contempt and heedlessness of God's blessings (niʿam).
Bodily Treatment (ʿIlāj al-Jism)
Diet Barley water; pomegranate juice cooled; avoid meat in excess; rose conserve
Herbs Borage (lisan al-thawr); violet; camphor dissolved in rosewater
Phlebotomy Venesection at median cubital vein, 200 dirhams — if pulse permits
Rest Cool chamber; moderate movement; avoid excitement and heated debate
Spiritual Treatment (ʿIlāj al-Rūḥ)
Dhikr سُبْحَانَ اللَّه — 100× at dawn, reflecting on one's created nature
Murāqaba Contemplate the graves of kings; recall that all return to dust
Service Serve the poor with hands; eat one meal with the destitute weekly
Fast Three days of voluntary fast; break with dates and water only
Map of the Qalb — Spiritual Zones
Nafs — Commanding Self
Appetites, anger, heedlessness; seat of the animal soul and bodily desires
Qalb — The Heart
The locus of faith and doubt; where light and darkness contend
Rūḥ — The Spirit
The divine breath; wisdom, love, contemplative peace
Sirr — The Secret
The innermost depth; pure receptivity to divine unveiling
