Muraqqa — The Assembled Album

The album leaf slides open on its hinge — emerging from the right edge as if the album binder's thread has been loosened. This modal is a leaf of the collection: bounded by crimson border bands, sealed at corners with gold roundels, its paper the colour of oxidized cream.

The Hinge Metaphor

In the Ottoman Muraqqa, pages are often mounted on a concertina binding — the album fold-opens, revealing calligraphy on one face and illumination on the other. This modal enacts that same turn.
