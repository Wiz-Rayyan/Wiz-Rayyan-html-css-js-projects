Cosmological Record — Year 13,800,000,000

The universe began 13.8 billion years ago from an infinitely dense singularity. In the first fraction of a second, space itself expanded faster than light — a period called inflation.

Everything you have ever seen, every atom in your body, every photon of light — all emerged from a single point of near-infinite density and temperature.

Now watch what happens when time reverses. The Big Crunch is the mirror of creation: gravity wins, expansion halts, and all matter collapses back toward the origin.
