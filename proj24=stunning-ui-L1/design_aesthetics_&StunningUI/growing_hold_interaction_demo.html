Growing hold interaction demo — long-press a card to reveal action petal buttons

Long-press any card to grow it and reveal petal actions.
Slide to a petal and release to trigger it.

Hold a card for 500ms…
1 · press Progress ring draws; card pulses gently.
2 · bloom After 500ms, card scales up; petals spring out.
3 · slide Drag finger over a petal — it glows to highlight.
4 · release Highlighted action fires; burst collapse.
