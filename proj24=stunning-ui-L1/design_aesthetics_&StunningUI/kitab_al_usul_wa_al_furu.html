    Working Example (Wine → Beer):
    Aṣl: Wine is ḥarām by explicit Qurʾānic verse (5:90).
    ʿIlla: Its intoxicating property (iskār) is the operative cause.
    Farʿ: Beer shares this ʿilla — therefore beer is also ḥarām by Qiyās.
    Sarakhsī (al-Mabsūṭ): "Whatever intoxicates in large quantities is prohibited in small quantities."

    Ijmāʿ — Scholarly Consensus Through Centuries
    Definition of Ijmāʿ (Sarakhsī, al-Mabsūṭ):
    The unanimous agreement of the mujtahidīn of the Muslim community in any age after the Prophet's death on a legal ruling. Hanafī scholars accept both explicit (ṣarīḥ) and tacit (sukūtī) consensus. Ijmāʿ abrogates no Qurʾānic text but can specify and restrict its application.

    Iftāʾ — Fatwā Generator (الإفتاء)
    FATWĀ — Mahr (Dower) Non-Payment

    The mahr is a financial obligation (dain) upon the husband and constitutes the wife's full right. In Hanafī law (Sarakhsī, al-Mabsūṭ, Kitāb al-Nikāḥ), the unpaid mahr after consummation becomes a confirmed debt (dain muʾakkad). The wife may seek judicial enforcement (qaḍāʾ) or withhold conjugal rights until payment (habs al-nafs).

    The Qāḍī must issue a decree ordering payment within 30 days, failing which the marriage may be dissolved by judicial order at the wife's request.
    Source: Sarakhsī, al-Mabsūṭ, Bk. V, Ch. al-Nikāḥ § Mahr al-Mithl · Qurʾān 4:4, 2:237 · Ḥanafī school position
