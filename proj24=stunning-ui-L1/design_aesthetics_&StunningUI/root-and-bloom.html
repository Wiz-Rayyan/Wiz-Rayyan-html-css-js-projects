From field to jar, in five weeks

Nothing here is instant, and we've stopped apologizing for that.

Week 1 — Harvest at peak

Pickers work at dawn, when oils in the leaf are most concentrated.

Week 2 — Sun-cure

Slow drying in open air, never a dehydrator, to keep the plant's structure intact.

Week 3 — Cold infusion

Steeped in oil at room temperature for ten days — heat would flatten the scent.

Week 4 — Small-batch blend

Batches capped at 40 jars, mixed and poured by hand.

Week 5 — Rests, then ships

Every jar cures a final week on the shelf before it leaves the workshop.
