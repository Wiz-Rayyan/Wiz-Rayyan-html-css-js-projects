On Arithmetic

Mathematical Sciences · Part I
The unity of number is the root of all existence. From one flows all multiplicity, and to one does all return. Number is the form of forms.
Mineral ابجد = 9
GLOSS Unity precedes being. One is not a number but the source of number.

On Music

Mathematical Sciences · Part I
The harmony of the spheres is the archetype of earthly music. Intervals of 4:3 (fourth), 3:2 (fifth), and 2:1 (octave) govern cosmic motion.
Vegetal موسيقى = 256

On Generation of Animals

Natural Sciences · Part II
Life arises when the four humours achieve proper balance. The animal soul perceives, moves, and desires — distinguishing it from the mineral and vegetal.
