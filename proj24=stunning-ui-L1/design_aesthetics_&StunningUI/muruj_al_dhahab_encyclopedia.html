The Magnetic Mountain
3rd Climate · Coast of Somalia
A great black mountain rises from the sea near the Zanj coast, possessed of such powerful attraction that it draws out all iron nails from ships that sail too close, causing them to fall apart. Sailors must use wooden pegs on vessels bound for these waters. Al-Masʿūdī records that merchants detour 300 farsakhs to avoid it.
