Atomic Notes

Overview

An [[Atomic Note]] contains exactly one idea, making it linkable, reusable, and composable. Unlike wiki entries, atomic notes serve the author's thinking — not a reader.

key insight
Connections between notes matter more than the notes themselves. A note without links is an orphan.
Core principles

Notes should be in your own words, not copied

Each note has one [[concept]] or argument

Titles are [[claim statements]], not topic labels

Always link to [[related ideas]]

Vs. traditional notes

Most note-taking buries ideas in long documents. The [[Zettelkasten]] method inverts this: small notes, densely connected, form an emergent second brain.

"A slip box that gets filled with ideas over decades can generate a lifetime's work of insight."
