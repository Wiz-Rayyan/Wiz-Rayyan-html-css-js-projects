توحيد Divine Unity
God is absolutely one — no multiplicity of attributes distinct from His essence. The Muʿtazila deny that divine attributes (knowledge, power, will) subsist as real entities alongside God, as this would imply shirk. Attributes are identical with the divine essence.
Rational proof: If divine attributes were distinct eternal entities, we would have multiple co-eternal beings — but only God is eternal. Therefore attributes cannot be distinct from essence. ∴ Absolute unity.
Ashʿarī counter: Attributes must subsist as real properties, else predicating them of God becomes meaningless. "God is knowing" implies a real attribute of knowledge.
