The space resists description. Every attempt to draw a straight line results in a curve. The horizon wraps. Two paths set out in parallel directions have already met somewhere behind you — you simply have not yet walked far enough to see it.

Triangles here contain more than 180 degrees of interior angle. The excess scales with the area enclosed. A triangle the size of this page contains 247 degrees. A triangle the size of a continent — we do not speak of it.
