Spaced Repetition
2024-03-15 5 min read 9 links
concept
Reviewing information at increasing intervals to exploit the forgetting curve — the rate at which memory decays without reinforcement.
How it works

The [[Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve]] shows that without review, ~70% of new information is lost within 24 hours. [[SRS algorithms]] like SM-2 schedule reviews just before forgetting occurs.
