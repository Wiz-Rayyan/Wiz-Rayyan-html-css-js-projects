The colour between blue and green
the Welsh have named it —
sky and sea and living grass
all one aching word.

The Threshold
I stood once at a door
in a country I had dreamed —
the smoke still rising from
a hearth that was not mine.
anon., Pembrokeshire
Cartref
Home, they say, is where
the light falls in a certain way
through window-glass you cannot
find again.
R. S. Thomas, paraphr.
Stone Memory
The standing stone has stood
three thousand years of rain —
and still remembers the hands
that set it, warm.
trad., Ynys Môn
