The Atelier

Every movement passes through eleven hands before it earns the Meridian name.

We build fewer than four hundred watches a year, not because we can't build more, but because a movement rushed is a movement that will fail its owner at the wrong hour.

Cases are milled from a single billet of 316L steel or 18k gold. Dials are lacquered in small batches, so no two years of the same reference are ever quite identical.
