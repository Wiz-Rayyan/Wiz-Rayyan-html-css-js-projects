Cloud-Cartouche Cards

🌹
The Rose
"Last night, the rose whispered secrets
to the nightingale at dawn —
speak softly, beloved, lest the garden hear."
The central metaphor of Hafiz: the rose as the divine beloved, the nightingale as the yearning soul enraptured by unattainable beauty.
از دیوان حافظ شیرازی
🌙
The Night Garden
"Pour the wine while yet the crescent hangs
above the jasmine wall —
morning comes for those who sleep, not lovers."
The night garden — a protected world of wine, poetry, and longing, shielded from the rationalists of the day by the vault of heaven itself.
باغ شب — غزل شماره ٧
🍷
The Cupbearer
"O Saki, bring the cup — not for forgetting,
but to remember more clearly
the face that lit this garden into fire."
The sāqi (cupbearer) is both human beloved and divine intermediary — the figure through whom wine flows as grace, and gaze as revelation.
ساقی‌نامه — مجلس بزم
