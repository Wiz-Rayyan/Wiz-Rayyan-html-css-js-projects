Soft Evidence
of Being Here

Made from the edges of attention, where certainty softens into something closer to feeling.

I —
Peripheral Light
What we notice when we stop looking directly. The corner of vision knows things the centre does not.
II —
Fugitive Moments
Long exposures of ordinary rooms. Blur is not failure. It is time made visible, motion becoming texture.
III —
Without Glasses
The world at morning, unresolved. Shapes before they become objects. Colour before it becomes meaning.
IV —
Rain Studies
Seen through wet glass, from inside. The world twice removed. Every drop a small lens, a small distortion.
