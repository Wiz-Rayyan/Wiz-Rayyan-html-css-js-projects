Why it's slow

We build one bench a day, on purpose.

Most furniture is cut fast and glued to hide it. Ours is joined with mortise and tenon, which takes a full day per piece and needs no glue to hold — so it can be taken apart and repaired, not replaced.

Oak and ash sourced from managed Danish forests, one tree lot per collection.
Every joint is cut and fitted by hand, no fasteners, no veneer.
A 25-year guarantee — we'll refinish or repair any piece we made.
